## 0.1.1 - 2026-11-10

Atlas now shows up when you search for it from the Start menu.
Setup puts Atlas on your desktop too, and Settings can take it off again.
Atlas chat replies are shorter, with a plain estimate line.
Lists and filters no longer hide behind each other in a small window.
